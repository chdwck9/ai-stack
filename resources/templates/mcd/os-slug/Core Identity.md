## What this section is for

Establishes why this thing exists, for whom, and what success looks like. An agent needs this to understand intent before touching structure or mechanics.

---

## Building Blocks

_Answer each question. Delete the instructions once populated._

### Purpose / Reason for Existence

_Ask: "What would be missing from the world if this didn't exist?" or "What problem does this solve that nothing else does?"_

_Verify: Test the answer against a devil's advocate — if a competitor or substitute could replace it without loss, sharpen the answer._

### North Star / Mission Statement

_Ask: "In 10 years, if this succeeds completely, what is true?" Derive a one-sentence mission from that answer._

_Verify: Every decision made for this system should be traceable back to this statement._

[ANSWER]

### Target Audience

_Ask: "Who is the primary user? Who is the buyer (if different)? Who is affected but not a user?"_

_Interview: Talk to 3–5 actual or representative users. Observation: Check existing analytics, support tickets, or sales notes for signals._
