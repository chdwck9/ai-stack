# Wayfinder

This is a significant, weighty process. A season is a meaningful chapter of Richard's life — not a quarter, not a sprint. When this skill is invoked, treat it accordingly. Move slowly, ask carefully, and don't rush toward the Notion writes. The conversation is the work. Read Richard OS before asking a single question. Use what's already known as a foundation. Don't ask Richard to repeat things the OS already holds.

## Steps

Copy these steps verbatim into the task list before starting. A skipped step stays in the list marked `skip: <reason>` — never dropped silently.

### Step 0 — Pre-flight

Before any interview questions:

1. Fetch and read: Richard OS parent index, @My-Bonds, @My-Craft, @My-Edge (pillar pages + current priorities/anti-goals), @My-Shadow, @My-Mission, @My-Vision (for season framing context), any previous Wayfinder Reports in the OS.

1. Synthesise into a pre-flight brief: current season label and framing, current pillar priority order, any stated goals/anti-goals/commitments from the last session.

1. Present the pre-flight brief to Richard. Ask: *"Here's what I understand about where you are. What's changed, what's shifted, or what's no longer true since this was last captured?"*

1. Let Richard's answer shape what questions are most important in the steps ahead. Do not proceed through a fixed list if the answer makes certain questions irrelevant.


---


### Step 1 — Season Framing

Establish the macro context. One question at a time. Listen for what Richard doesn't say as much as what he does.

1. The current season has a label — does it still feel accurate? What metaphor or word would you use for where you actually are right now?

1. What would it look and feel like to move into the next season? Not the goals — the felt sense. What would be different about your days, your energy, your relationships?

1. What is the end game? If you trace the arc forward — what does the end of the following season look like for you as a whole person, not just professionally?

Reflect back a **Season Summary** (4–6 sentences): current season label and honest characterisation, what signals the next transition, the long-horizon end game.

**Pause. Do not proceed to Step 2 until Richard approves the Season Summary or revises it.**


---


### Step 2 — Pillar Assessment

Work through Bonds, Craft, and Edge in current priority order. One pillar at a time.

For each pillar:

1. What does this pillar actually contain right now — what's in it, what's active, what's been neglected?

1. If you had to give your performance in this pillar an honest score out of 10, what would it be and why?

1. Where is this pillar creating friction with the others — where do they compete, where do they support each other?

After all three, present a **Pillar Assessment Summary**: priority ranking (confirm or propose adjustment), score per pillar with one-line honest rationale, interaction tensions.

Ask: does this ranking still feel right, or does something need to move?

**Pause. Wait for approval or revision before Step 3.**


---


### Step 3 — Pillar Goal-Setting

For each pillar in priority order. One pillar at a time. Do not move to the next until the current one is approved.

For each pillar:

1. What is the **one core goal** for this pillar this season — the single thing that would make you say "yes, this was a good season for [Bonds/Craft/Edge]"? If Richard offers multiple, push back: "If you could only achieve one, which one?"

1. What are **3–9 projects or rituals** that would move you toward that goal?

  - Project = a discrete outcome with a start and end

  - Ritual = a recurring practice with a cadence

  For each: Name, Type (Project/Ritual), Cadence (Rituals only: Daily/Weekly/Monthly), why it connects to the core goal, rough timing (projects).

1. What is the **one non-negotiable anti-goal** for this pillar — the thing you must not do this season, even if it seems attractive or justified?

Present a **Pillar Goal Summary** after each pillar. Wait for explicit approval.


---


### Step 4 — Bottom-Up Sense-Check

With all three pillars assessed and goals set, work upward. This is the integrity check.

1. Looking across everything you've committed to — does it fit in a life with two young children, a full-time Platform PM role, and finite energy? What's actually going to give?

1. Does the priority ranking from Step 2 still hold, or does what you've committed to in Step 3 reveal a different order?

1. Does the Season Summary from Step 1 still feel accurate? Or does something need updating?

1. Name one thing you've said you'll do that you already know, honestly, you probably won't. Let's remove or reschedule it now rather than carry it as guilt.

Revise Season Summary and Pillar Assessment if needed. Get explicit final approval before proceeding to Step 5.

### Step 6 — Propagate to Notion

Confirm with Richard before each batch of writes. Execute in sequence.

**6a. Save the Wayfinder Report** — Create a new page in Richard OS titled "Wayfinder Report [Season] [Year]". Content: the full approved report in Markdown.

**6b. Update Richard OS pillar pages (Bonds, Craft, Edge)** — For each pillar: update the "Wayfinder Goals" section with core goal and anti-goal; update the "Priorities This Season" list. Confirm before writing each pillar.

**6c. Update Richard OS parent index** — Update Seasonal Priority Order, Seasonal Anti-Goals, Seasonal Constraints if anything changed.

**6d. Update Shadow pages** — @My-Mission: update seasonal mission statement if it changed. @My-Vision: update "What he is building toward" if it changed. Surgical updates only — do not rewrite full pages.

**6e. Update agent mandates** *(when agents exist)* — For each agent whose pillar scope or seasonal priority changed: fetch the agent page, update the seasonal focus section only. Skip if agents have not yet been built.

**6f. Write to Ship It DB** — Present the full list of items to be written before executing any:

For each confirmed **Project**: Name, Level = Project, Status = Do next (imminent) or Backlog (later in season), Priority = #1 (Pillar 1), #3 (Pillar 2), #5 (Pillar 3), Live = relevant pillar's Live tag, When? = stated timing if provided.

For each confirmed **Ritual**: Name, Level = Ritual, Status = Doing, Cadence = Daily/Weekly/Monthly as stated, Live = relevant pillar's Live tag, Priority = #1/#3/#5 per pillar priority.

Confirm the full list. Execute writes only after Richard approves.

**6g. Flag stale Ship It items** — Search Ship It for active projects and rituals that conflict with any new anti-goal, were named and removed in Step 4 Q4, or are clearly from a previous season. Present the flagged list. Richard decides: Archive, Reschedule, or Keep. Never archive autonomously.

## Boundaries

- Must Not begin the interview without reading Richard OS first (Step 0 is mandatory)

- Must Not ask Richard to repeat information the OS already holds

- Must Not combine steps or move ahead without explicit approval at each pause point

- Must Not write anything to Notion until Step 5 report is fully approved

- Must Not rewrite full agent pages — surgical mandate updates only

- Must Not create Ship It entries without presenting the full list for confirmation first

- Must Not archive Ship It items autonomously — present, let Richard decide

- Must Not treat Shadow as a wayfinding pillar — it is input context, not assessed output

- Must Not treat this as a quarterly process — it runs at genuine season transitions only

- Must Always reflect back a summary at the end of each step before moving on

- Must Always ask one question at a time within each step

- Must Always honour the seasonal priority order (Bonds first, Craft second, Edge third) unless Richard explicitly reorders in Step 2 or Step 4
